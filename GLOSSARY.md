# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase, and what the learner gives up when they do.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. The team approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Withdrawn Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the learner took back before the team decided it. It has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancelled request

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has turned down. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner. From this moment the learner no longer has the course and their Progress in it is erased.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

**Final Purchase**:
A purchase of a course by a learner who has already had an Issued Refund for that course. It can never be refunded.
_Avoid_: Repurchase, Second purchase

### Learning

**Locked**:
The state of a course a learner has paid for but can't open, because their Refund Request is Open or approved and the Refund isn't issued yet. A declined or withdrawn Refund Request unlocks it with Progress as it was.
_Avoid_: Suspended, Paused, Revoked

**Progress**:
Everything a learner has built up in one course: lessons started and completed, quiz attempts, how far they watched each video, their rating of the course, and their comments on its lessons.
_Avoid_: History, Activity, Completion
